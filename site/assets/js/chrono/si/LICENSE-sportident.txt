Chronoz — SPORTident support (src/si/, test/t_si.mjs)

The SPORTident protocol codec, the card layouts, the readout flow and the test data were ported from three
MIT-licensed projects. Their copyright notices and the licence they share follow.

1. sportident.js — https://github.com/allestuetsmerweh/sportident.js
   Copyright (c) 2018 Simon Hatt
   Ported: the command constants and the CRC (constants.ts, siProtocol.ts), the date and card-number conversions,
   the cards' storage layouts and the card holder's fields (SiCard/types/*.ts), the station's system data layout,
   modes and models (SiStation/BaseSiStation.ts, ISiStation.ts), the readout settings (SiStation/SiMainStation.ts),
   and test data (siProtocol.test.ts, SiCard/types/*Examples.ts, SiStation/siStationExamples.ts).

2. lool-ing/sportident — https://github.com/lool-ing/sportident
   Copyright (c) 2026 Jonas Hagberg and the LooL contributors
   (itself ported in large part from sportident.js)
   Ported: the layout of a punch a station sends by itself (autosend.ts), the number ranges of the card
   families (SiCard/cardTypeFromNumber.ts), the retries of a card's read (SiStation/SiMainStation.ts).

3. GecoSI — https://github.com/sdenier/GecoSI
   Copyright (c) 2013 Simon Denier
   Ported: the start-up and readout sequence and the speed fallback (internal/SiDriver.java, SiDriverState.java,
   SiMessage.java), the half-day bit, the control codes above 255 and the order of a card 6's blocks
   (dataframe/*.java), and the recorded frames and card dumps of its tests (src/test/net/gecosi/
   SiMessageFixtures.java, dataframe/*Test.java) — in which the card holders' names, club, e-mail and address
   were replaced by invented text.
